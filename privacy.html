SMS Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Sai Perchard’s Houston SMS program handles information associated with text messaging.

Information collected

When you text Houston, the program may collect your phone number, message content, delivery information, and related timestamps.

How information is used

This information is used only to provide requested conversational replies, operate and secure the service, troubleshoot delivery, and honor opt-out requests.

Sharing

Mobile information, text messaging originator opt-in data, and consent are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information may be processed by communications and technology providers solely as necessary to deliver and operate the requested messaging service.

Retention and security

Messaging records are retained only as reasonably needed to operate the service, maintain security, and meet legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but SMS is not end-to-end encrypted; do not send passwords, authentication codes, full payment-card numbers, Social Security numbers, or other highly sensitive information.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop messages. Reply HELP for assistance. You may also request access to or deletion of your messaging information by emailing sai@perchard.com.

Contact
