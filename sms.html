Houston SMS

Houston is Sai Perchard’s private household assistant. The SMS program provides conversational replies and household-coordination assistance to family members who initiate a conversation.

How to opt in

Open the SMS consent form, enter your name and mobile number, select the initially unchecked consent checkbox, and submit. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

The form records consent only; it does not send a text or grant access to Houston. Sai separately approves family access and privately shares the Houston number. Approved family members then text Houston to begin their conversation.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email sai@perchard.com.

This program does not send marketing messages or unsolicited outreach.
