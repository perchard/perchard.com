Houston SMS

Houston is Sai Perchard’s private household assistant. The SMS program provides conversational replies and household-coordination assistance to family members who initiate a conversation.

How to opt in

After Sai gives you the Houston phone number directly, text that number to begin. By initiating a text, you consent to receive conversational replies from Houston for Sai Perchard. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

  • Reply STOP to opt out.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email sai@perchard.com.

This program does not send marketing messages or unsolicited outreach.
