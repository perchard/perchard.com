Houston SMS Terms

Houston is Sai Perchard’s private household assistant. These terms apply to conversational SMS messages sent to family members who initiate a conversation with Houston.

Consent and eligibility

After Sai provides the Houston phone number, you may opt in by sending a text to that number. By initiating a text, you consent to receive conversational replies relating to your requests and household coordination. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your conversations. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan.

Opt out and support

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or email sai@perchard.com. After opting out, you may receive a final confirmation message. You may opt in again by sending a new message after asking Sai to restore access.

Service availability

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. The service may be changed or discontinued, and delivery is not guaranteed.
