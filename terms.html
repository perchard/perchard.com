Houston SMS Terms

Houston is Sai Perchard’s private household assistant. These terms apply to conversational SMS messages sent to family members who initiate a conversation with Houston.

Consent and eligibility

Family members opt in through the SMS consent form linked from Houston SMS: enter your name and mobile number, select the initially unchecked SMS consent checkbox, and submit. This records consent to conversational replies relating to your requests and household coordination. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Submitting the form does not grant access to Houston or trigger a text message. Sai separately verifies eligibility, approves access, and privately shares the Houston number; approved family members then initiate their conversation.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies based on your conversations. Message and data rates may apply under your mobile plan.

Opt out and support

Reply STOP at any time to opt out. Reply HELP for help, or email sai@perchard.com. After opting out, you may receive a final confirmation message. To request re-enrollment, submit the consent form again and ask Sai to review and restore access; submitting the form alone does not override a STOP request.

Service availability

Wireless carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. The service may be changed or discontinued, and delivery is not guaranteed.
